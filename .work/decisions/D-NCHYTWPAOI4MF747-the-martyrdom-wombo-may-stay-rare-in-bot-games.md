# D-NCHYTWPAOI4MF747 The Martyrdom wombo may stay rare in bot games

## Context
The w8 criterion asked for the pull, Martyrdom, Furnace Heart wombo on most seeds. The 8d batch saw it in about 1 game in 10 (Director mode); schedule-mode seeds saw none. The causes are timing: the Lash's clump disperses within the lead time, Martyrdom's cooldown, and Furnace overheat.

## Decision
B (2026-11-02): accept the wombo as rare. No timing retune or harder bot coordination for M2.

## Why
A rare, memorable payoff fits the game; forcing it costs tuning churn before v1.0. The alternatives were to retune the Lash lead or Clumped duration, or to make the bots coordinate harder.

## Consequences
Low wombo counts in reporter batches are not a regression on their own. Revisit with human play.
