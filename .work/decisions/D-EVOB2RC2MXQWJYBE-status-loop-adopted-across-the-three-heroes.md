# D-EVOB2RC2MXQWJYBE Status loop adopted across the three heroes

## Context
Wave 6 design checkpoint (2026-11-01): B answered the questions on the M2 kits and rules. Pillar 2 asks for a loop, not a chain.

## Decision
Adopted: Suppressed goes to the Psyker, Clumped to the Penitent and Gunner, and Exposed (applied by the Penitent's Chain of Penance) to the Gunner.

## Why
Each hero both sets up and cashes in, so the team has to coordinate.

## Consequences
Nothing applies Exposed until the Penitent kit lands. Nothing applies Suppressed after Pavise replaced Suppressing Fire; see the Pavise decision.
