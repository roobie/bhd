# D-ZQ7UANKFTPZIMMZW M2 kits are reduced first

## Context
Wave 6 design checkpoint (2026-11-01): B answered the questions on the M2 kits and rules. B was asked for full kits or reduced kits first.

## Decision
Penitent: Chain of Penance (taunt plus Exposed) and Martyrdom, with Pain. Psyker: Gravitic Lash (pull plus Clumped) with Warp and Discharge backlash. Gunner: full kit with Pavise. Full kits come after M2.

## Why
Reduced kits get M2 playable sooner and still exercise the status loop.

## Consequences
The Suppressed cash-in (Warp Lance) and Rift backlash are not in M2.
